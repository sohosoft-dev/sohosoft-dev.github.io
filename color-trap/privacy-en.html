Privacy Policy

App: Color Trap: DON'T READ! · Package com.sohosoft.colortrap
Developer: SohoSoft · Effective: November 6, 2026

1. Information we collect directly

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or accounts. The app has no sign-up or login.

Best scores, play counts, Daily Challenge history, and settings (sound, haptics, language) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Information processed by third-party services

ServiceDataPurpose
Google Firebase Analytics App instance ID, game start/end, score, combo, accuracy, reaction times for sampled questions, language / app version / OS version, approximate region (derived from IP by Google) Balancing difficulty and improving the app (anonymous statistics). Advertising ID collection is disabled.
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactions Showing and measuring interstitial ads between games

Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Your choices

  • Turn off anonymous stats: Settings → "Share anonymous stats" stops Firebase Analytics.
  • Personalized ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads).
  • Delete on-device records: clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Retention

We do not store any personal information. Firebase Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months under our Firebase settings.

5. Children

This app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. International transfer

Data processed through Firebase Analytics and AdMob may be processed on servers of Google LLC in the United States.
